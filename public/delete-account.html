⚠️ Account Deletion
Overview

🗑️ Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Dine Time account directly from within the app. This action is irreversible — once deleted, your account and personal data cannot be recovered.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Your login access will be revoked immediately. If you are a restaurant owner, your restaurant listing will also be removed.
In-App Method (Recommended)

📱 Delete From the App

Follow these steps inside the Dine Time app:

1
Open the Dine Time app on your Android device
2
Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
3
Scroll down to the Privacy & Security section
4
Tap Delete Account — a confirmation sheet will appear
5
Review what will be deleted, then tap Continue
6
Enter your account password to confirm your identity and tap Delete Forever
7
Your account will be permanently deleted and you will be signed out immediately
Data Deletion

📦 What Gets Deleted

👤
Your profile — name, email address, phone number, profile photo
📍
Your saved addresses and location preferences
❤️
Your saved favourite restaurants
🔐
Your Firebase authentication account — login access revoked immediately
🏪
If you are a restaurant owner — your restaurant listing and all associated data
ℹ️ Kept for Legal Compliance
💳
Payment transaction records — anonymized only, no personal identity attached. Kept to comply with financial and tax regulations.
Behind the Scenes

⚙️ What Happens When You Delete

When you confirm deletion, here is what the app does automatically:

1️⃣
Removes profile data from our database
2️⃣
Removes your restaurant listing (if you are an owner)
3️⃣
Anonymizes any payment records — replacing your name and contact with "Deleted User"
4️⃣
Permanently closes and removes your login account from Firebase Authentication
