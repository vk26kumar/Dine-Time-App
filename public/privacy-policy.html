Data Usage

🎯 How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your Dine Time account
  • To show nearby restaurants based on your location
  • To process table reservations and bookings
  • To handle payments securely through Razorpay
  • To enable restaurant owners to list and manage their establishments
  • To display your booking history and favourite restaurants
  • To send booking confirmations and support responses
  • To improve app performance using Firebase Analytics

Security

🔒 Data Security

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS
  • Payment data is handled exclusively by Razorpay — we never store card or bank details
  • Sensitive tokens and credentials are stored using Expo SecureStore (device-level encryption)
  • Firebase security rules restrict data access to authenticated users only
  • Profile images are stored securely on Cloudinary with access controls
Location Data

📍 Location Usage

Dine Time requests access to your precise location (foreground and background) to show restaurants near you. Location data is used only to enhance your discovery experience and is never sold to third parties.

You can revoke location permission at any time from your device's Settings → Apps → Dine Time → Permissions.

Your Rights

✋ Your Rights & Choices

  • Edit your profile — Update your name, email, phone, and photo anytime from the Profile screen
  • Delete your account — Permanently remove your account and all associated data using the "Delete Account" option in Privacy & Security settings
  • Revoke permissions — Disable location or media access from your device settings at any time
  • Data requests — Contact us at dinetimeteam@gmail.com to request a copy or deletion of your data
Payments

💳 Payment & Refund Policy

All reservation payments processed through Dine Time are non-refundable. Please confirm all booking details — date, time, guest count, and restaurant — carefully before completing your payment.

For payment disputes or issues, contact our support team within 48 hours of your booking.

Age Policy

👶 Children's Privacy

Dine Time is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at dinetimeteam@gmail.com.
